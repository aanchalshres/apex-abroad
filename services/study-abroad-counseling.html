Our 5-Pillar Counseling Matrix

1. Academic Fit

GPA, backlogs, relevant prerequisites, and degree equivalency checks.

2. Budget Planning

Realistic tuition, living costs, bank balance requirements, and loan eligibility.

3. Career Outlook

Employability, STEM designations, and industry demand across target regions.

4. Visa Risk Analysis

SOP viability, financial documentation strength, and current refusal trends.

5. Post-Study Rights
