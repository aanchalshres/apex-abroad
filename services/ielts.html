Course Overview

Cracking IELTS with Precision & Practice

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is the global benchmark for international higher education in the UK, Australia, Canada, USA, and New Zealand. At Apex Abroad, our 6-week intensive training covers all four modules: Listening, Reading, Writing (Task 1 & Task 2), and Speaking.

With small batch sizes capped at 15 students, our certified instructors give line-by-line feedback on your academic essays and run real-time audio mock speaking interviews.

Curriculum

Comprehensive Module Training

1. Listening

Master accent recognition (British, Aussie, American), keyword tracking, and distraction traps across 4 sections.

2. Reading

Skimming and scanning techniques, True/False/Not Given mastery, and time management for 3 dense passages.

3. Writing

Cohesion, coherence, lexical resources, and grammatical range for Task 1 data reports and Task 2 argumentative essays.

4. Speaking

1-on-1 interview simulations, cue card brainstorming frameworks, fluency exercises, and pronunciation clinics.
