Terms & Conditions

Guidelines and mutual agreements regarding educational counseling and application services.

1. Scope of Services

Apex Abroad provides education consulting, university admission application facilitation, test preparation training (IELTS, PTE, SAT), and visa document checking. Final decisions on admissions and visas rest exclusively with the respective universities and foreign government immigration departments.

2. Authenticity of Documentation

Students are solely responsible for ensuring the authenticity of all academic transcripts, test certificates, and financial bank statements presented. Submission of fraudulent documentation is strictly prohibited and grounds for immediate termination of representation.

3. Fees and Refunds

Educational counseling is provided free of charge. Course enrollment fees for IELTS, PTE, and SAT cover course materials, instructor access, and mock tests. Third-party statutory fees (such as embassy visa fees and biometric charges) are non-refundable.
