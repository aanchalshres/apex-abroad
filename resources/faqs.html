Admissions & Study Gap Requirements

For undergraduate applicants (+2 completed), a study gap of 1 to 2 years is generally acceptable if justified with test preparation or relevant volunteering. For Master's applicants, study gaps of up to 5 to 7 years are fully acceptable provided you submit continuous work experience letters, salary bank statements, tax clearance, and CIT/SSF records.
We recommend starting 6 to 9 months before your target intake (e.g. for September intakes, start in January/February; for February/March intakes, start in July/August). This allows sufficient time for IELTS/PTE preparation, university offer letter processing, financial documentation, and visa lodging.

Financial Sponsorship & Bank Loans in Nepal

Primary sponsors include immediate family members: Father, Mother, Real Brothers/Sisters, and Paternal/Maternal Grandparents. Acceptable financial documents include education loans from 'Class A' commercial banks of Nepal, fixed deposit receipts held for the required duration, and verifiable annual family income (business profits, agriculture, rental, or salary with municipal ward tax verification).
Education loans and bank balances must originate from Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) approved 'Class A' Commercial Banks (such as Nabil Bank, Nepal Investment Mega Bank, Rastriya Banijya Bank, Sanima Bank, Global IME, etc.). Co-operative society accounts and finance company loans are generally not accepted for visa financial evidence.
For Australia (Subclass 500), you must show 1 year of tuition fees + 1 year of living costs (approx. AUD $29,710) + travel allowance (approx. AUD $2,000). For the UK, you must show remaining 1st year tuition fees + living costs (£1,334/month for London or £1,023/month outside London for 9 months) held in a bank account for 28 consecutive days.

Test Preparation (IELTS, PTE, SAT)

For Bachelor's degrees, universities generally require an IELTS score of 6.0 overall (no band less than 5.5) or a PTE Academic score of 50-58 overall. For Master's degrees, universities require an IELTS score of 6.5 overall (no band less than 6.0) or a PTE score of 58-65 overall. Nursing, Law, and Medicine programs may require higher scores (IELTS 7.0+).

Visas, Work Rights & Reapplications

The Australian GS requirement and Canadian Statement of Purpose (SOP) assess your genuine intent to study abroad. You must detail your academic background, explain why you chose the specific course and university, demonstrate research into Nepal's local employment opportunities versus international career prospects, and show strong economic ties returning to your home country.
