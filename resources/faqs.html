ANSWERS TO COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, accurate answers regarding study gaps, financial sponsorship from Nepal, English testing, and visa processing times.

How much study gap is acceptable for Australia and Canada?

+
For undergraduate applicants (+2 completed), a study gap of 1 to 2 years is typically acceptable if justified with language preparation or relevant volunteering. For postgraduate applicants, study gaps of up to 5 to 7 years are fully acceptable provided you submit continuous work experience letters, tax clearance, and salary payslips.

Who can sponsor my education from Nepal?

+
Direct family members (Father, Mother, Real Brothers/Sisters, and Grandparents) are recognized as primary sponsors. Acceptable financial documents include education loans from 'Class A' commercial banks of Nepal, fixed deposit receipts, or documented family business/agricultural annual income with local municipal ward verification.

Does Apex Abroad charge any fees for counseling?

+
No! All initial educational counseling, university course shortlisting, eligibility assessments, and document checklists are 100% free of charge. You only pay for test preparation materials and third-party fees (such as embassy visa fees and medical tests).

Can I reapply if my visa was previously rejected?

+
Yes. Many students with prior refusals come to Apex Abroad. We order ATIP GCMS notes (for Canada) or review decision record letters (for Australia/USA), resolve the specific concerns highlighted by the visa officer, and submit an evidence-backed reapplication with high success rates.
