Comparison Guide #1

Australia vs. Canada for Nepali Students

Both nations are top choices for Nepali students. Australia offers warm weather, higher minimum wages (AUD $24.10/hr), and the Group of Eight prestige. Canada provides lower tuition fees, Express Entry immigration pathways, and 3-year PGWP options.

Compare Your Profile With Us

Quick Head-to-Head

  • Avg. Living Cost: Australia (AUD $29,710) vs Canada (CAD $20,635)
  • Work Rights: Australia (48 hrs/fortnight) vs Canada (24 hrs/wk)
  • Post-Study Visa: Australia (2-4 yrs) vs Canada (up to 3 yrs)
  • Weather: Moderate/Subtropical vs Continental/Snowy
Comparison Guide #2

United Kingdom vs. United States

The UK is famous for fast-track 1-year Master's programs that cut down costs, while the USA offers extensive campus funding, research assistantships, and 36-month STEM OPT work extensions.

Evaluate UK vs USA Options

Quick Head-to-Head

  • Master's Length: UK (1 Year) vs USA (2 Years)
  • Scholarships: UK (Bursaries £2k-£8k) vs USA (Up to 100% full-ride)
  • Work Extension: UK (2 yrs Graduate Route) vs USA (3 yrs STEM OPT)
  • Exams: UK (IELTS/PTE) vs USA (SAT/GRE + English)
