Privacy Policy

Your personal and academic data privacy is our highest priority.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for counseling, test preparation, or university application processing, Apex Abroad collects personal identification details (name, email, phone number, date of birth) and academic records (transcripts, certificates, English test scores, passport copies).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to submit university admission applications, process Genuine Student/visa documentation, communicate admission offers, and arrange pre-departure preparations. We never sell or lease student data to third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Security & Retention

All physical and digital documents are kept in encrypted repositories accessible only by authorized counselors handling your specific file. You may request deletion of your data once admissions and visa procedures are concluded.
