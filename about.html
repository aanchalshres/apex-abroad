OUR STORY & MISSION

Over 10 years of trusted guidance in Putalisadak, Kathmandu, empowering more than 5,000 students to gain admission and scholarships across the world's premier universities.

Who We Are

Empowering Ambitious Students To Excel Globally

Founded with a commitment to integrity and transparency, Apex Abroad has established itself as Nepal's most reliable international educational consultancy. Our seasoned counselors understand that studying abroad is not just about filing applications—it is a life-defining career investment.

From high-impact test preparation (IELTS, PTE, and SAT) to personalized university selection, visa interview coaching, and pre-departure accommodation assistance, we support students through every milestone.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To empower Nepali students with genuine information, personalized mentoring, and top-tier test preparation so they can unlock world-class higher education opportunities without financial or procedural distress.

Our Vision

To be the most ethical, results-oriented, and student-focused global consultancy in South Asia, recognized internationally by top universities for producing visionary scholars and leaders.

Our Values

Uncompromising integrity, student-first advocacy, rigorous compliance with global immigration laws, continuous training, and compassionate support at every stage of the student lifecycle.
