6-WEEK MASTERCLASS

IELTS Comprehensive Syllabus

A structured 6-week curriculum covering all IELTS question types with daily writing assessments and individual speaking interviews.

Weekly Curriculum Breakdown

Week 1: Foundations & Diagnostic Benchmark

Diagnostic baseline exam, overview of band descriptors, skimming & scanning in Reading Section 1, and introductory Speaking Part 1 patterns.

Week 2: Listening Strategies & Writing Task 1 (Academic)

Form completion, map labeling, and multiple-choice traps. Describing graphs, bar charts, line graphs, pie charts, and process diagrams.

Week 3: Advanced Reading & Speaking Cue Cards

Conquering True/False/Not Given, Heading Matching, and summary completion. Speaking Part 2 structured note-taking frameworks.

Week 4: Writing Task 2 Argumentative Essays

Opinion essays, Discussion essays, Problem/Solution formats, and Cause/Effect structures. Vocabulary enhancements and complex sentence syntax.

Week 5: Speaking Part 3 & Complex Academic Texts

Abstract philosophical discourse in Speaking Part 3. Scientific and social research passages in Reading Section 3.

Week 6: Intensive Mock Exam Simulations

Full-length timed computer & paper exam simulations, followed by private 1-on-1 feedback and personalized final polish.
